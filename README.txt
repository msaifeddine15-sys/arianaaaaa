TWO MEETINGS, ONE FOREVER — ANIMATED 2D LOVE STORY
===================================================

A small, video-like, animated English-language love story starring the
2D cartoon characters Saifeddine and Ariana. This is a WEBSITE that
plays like a film, NOT a downloadable .mp4 video.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Click "Watch our story" to start the animated movie.

CONTROLS
- Play/pause button or Space bar.
- Previous/next chapter buttons or Left/Right arrow keys.
- Click/drag timeline to seek.
- Voices on/off: uses built-in browser text-to-speech to speak the dialogue
  in English when supported. Voices can sound different on each device.
  Speech is optional; visible dialogue/subtitles always work.
- Music on/off: soft original synthesized tones (Web Audio API).
- Fullscreen button or F key.
- At the end, replay the film.

CONTENT
A fictionalized, affectionate dialogue inspired by a real couple:
Saifeddine from Morocco and Ariana from Switzerland, who met twice
before their Islamic marriage (Nikah), without a traditional wedding.
No specific meeting place or exact conversations are claimed as facts.

This site has no third-party JavaScript dependencies. Internet is optional:
Google Fonts may load when online but fallback fonts work offline.
The film animations, music, and spoken dialogue are generated in the browser.

EDITING THE STORY
All conversations and chapters are defined in the screenplay array at
the top of script.js. Modify the text inside the quotes as you like.
HTML/CSS/SVG characters are rendered using local code, not hosted images.
